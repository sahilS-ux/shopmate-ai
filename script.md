# ShopMate AI — Product Launch Demo Script

**Format:** Launch-style product film with live app footage  
**Target runtime:** 2:35–2:45  
**Narration pace:** Calm, measured; leave short pauses after the reveal and before the final line.  
**Direction:** Let the real interface carry the story. Use clean screen recordings, restrained cuts, readable cursor movement, and no background music. Do not show credentials, buyer account details, or private information.

## 0:00–0:18 — The moment of uncertainty

**Picture:** Black screen. Three short phrases appear, one at a time: “What fits my needs?” / “What fits my budget?” / “Which one should I choose?” Cut to the ShopMate AI home screen.

**Voiceover:**  
“Every shopping decision starts with a question. What fits my needs? What fits my budget? And which choice is right for me?”

**On-screen title:**  
“A clearer way to choose.”

## 0:18–0:35 — Product reveal

**Picture:** Hold on the hero area. Slowly move into the shopping prompt.

**Voiceover:**  
“Meet ShopMate AI: a shopping assistant that turns a product brief into a clear next step.”

**On-screen title:**  
“ShopMate AI”

## 0:35–1:10 — The AI shortlist

**Picture:** Type and submit: “I need a laptop under ₹60,000 with at least 16GB RAM for programming.” Show the understood requirements, matching catalogue products, and the recommendation explanation. Pause long enough to read the recommendation.

**Voiceover:**  
“I’ll start with what matters: a laptop, a budget, and the specifications I need. Gemini turns that request into structured requirements. ShopMate filters its catalogue, then recommends a matching product and explains the choice.”

**On-screen labels:**  
“UNDERSTANDS THE REQUEST” → “SHORTLISTS THE CATALOGUE” → “EXPLAINS A MATCH”

## 1:10–1:43 — Live product discovery

**Picture:** Move to Live Marketplace. Search for a related product. Show a live product result, retailer, original USD price, and approximate rupee value.

**Voiceover:**  
“For a wider view, ShopMate searches live product listings and retailer offers with Channel3. This integration currently uses Channel3’s default US market. Where an offer is in dollars, ShopMate also shows an approximate rupee comparison using a daily reference exchange rate. The retailer’s original price stays visible.”

**Small on-screen disclosure:**  
“Approximate conversion. Retailer price, shipping, taxes, and availability may differ.”

## 1:43–2:18 — Cart and payment demonstration

**Picture:** Add a sample-catalogue item to the cart. Show the INR cart total and the checkout disclosure. Briefly show the PayPal Sandbox approval, then the app’s confirmation and receipt. Keep buyer details out of frame.

**Voiceover:**  
“I can add a catalogue item to my cart and review its INR total. Then I can try the PayPal Sandbox checkout. This prototype sends a fixed one-dollar US Sandbox payment; it does not charge the cart total, and no real money is charged. After approval, ShopMate verifies the order and shows a receipt.”

**On-screen disclosure:**  
“PayPal Sandbox demo · Fixed $1 USD payment · No real money charged”

## 2:18–2:40 — Close

**Picture:** Return to the ShopMate AI hero. End on the product name and live demo URL.

**Voiceover:**  
“From a shopping brief to a considered choice, ShopMate AI brings recommendations, live product discovery, and a PayPal payment demonstration into one experience. ShopMate AI. Make your next product choice with clarity.”

**End card:**  
“ShopMate AI”  
“Try it: https://shopmate-ai-sahil.streamlit.app”

## Final edit checklist

- Keep the finished video under three minutes.
- Use the live app and real results; do not stage or fabricate API responses.
- Keep the Gemini, Channel3, and PayPal credentials off-screen.
- Preserve the USD price next to every approximate rupee conversion.
- Keep the fixed $1 Sandbox disclosure visible in the checkout segment.
- Upload the finished video publicly to YouTube and enable embedding before adding its link to Devpost.
